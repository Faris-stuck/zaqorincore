# Detections

ZaqorinCore ships with a catalog of Sigma-compatible detection rules.
The catalog is split into themed tracks so operators can navigate by
attack surface.

## Tracks

| Track | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| [Self-defense](self-defense/index.md) | Protects the ZaqorinCore server, agent, and WebUI from being probed, brute-forced, or silently disabled. 10 rules. |
